About Love Earth Cafe KD

Love Earth Cafe is a vegetarian cafe in Kota Damansara, offering delicious vegetarian and vegan dishes for different tastes and occasions.
Healthy & Thoughtful

Wholesome vegetarian and vegan dishes made with care, so you can enjoy food that tastes good and feels good.

A Taste for Every Craving

From local favourites and Lei Cha to sushi, noodles, rice and Western dishes, enjoy a variety of flavours all in one place.

16 Years of Love Earth

With years of experience, we continue to make vegetarian dining delicious, welcoming and easy to enjoy.

Mission & Concept

Vision

To produce and provide tasty, chemical-free food so families can stay healthy together.

Philosophy

Serves wholesome modern and traditional vegan meals without added MSG, emphasizing environmental care and compassion for animals.

Our Story

“I didn’t open Love Earth Cafe just to serve vegetarian food.”

I wanted to create a place where good food, good health and
a love for the earth could come together.

When I first started, vegetarian food was often seen as
simple or limited.

I wanted to show people that vegetarian food can be colourful, comforting and full of flavour.

Over the years, Love Earth Cafe has become more than a
restaurant to me.

It is a place where families gather, friends share a meal, and people discover that eating differently can still taste wonderfully familiar.

This little cafe started with a simple idea.
Today, it continues with the same heart.
